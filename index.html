The Firm

Most advisors have seen one side of the table. We’ve sat on four.

Every capital decision looks different depending on where you sit — and in private equity, the sponsor, the lender, and the management team are rarely sitting in the same seat. Our advice accounts for what the other side of your table is optimizing for.

Seat II — The Auditor

Seat III — The Operator

The CFO office

Seat IV — The Advisor

ADV—02

Transaction Readiness

Preparing management teams and financials for a sale, raise, or refinancing — the diligence questions answered before the other side asks them, for a cleaner process and a stronger exit.

Financial Transformation

Rebuilding the portfolio-company finance function — FP&A discipline, KPI frameworks, EBITDA enhancement — so the numbers hold up in the boardroom, the data room, and the exit story.

AI agents inside the finance function — close automation, reporting copilots, treasury and FP&A workflows — implemented by an advisor who has shipped these systems, without ripping out the platforms you already run on.
